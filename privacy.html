Privacy Policy

J S Computer Embroidery Works

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how J S Computer Embroidery Works collects, uses and protects customer information through the JS Embroidery application and website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Customer name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Blouse and saree photographs
  • Embroidery design photographs
  • Measurements
  • Order details and quantity
  • Order date and delivery date

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information provided by customers to process embroidery orders, understand design requirements, prepare measurements and communicate about orders.

4. Photos and Measurements

Customer photographs and measurements are used only for the requested embroidery work and order processing.

5. Email and Information Sharing

Order information submitted through the order form may be sent by email to the business for order management. We do not sell customer personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website and application may use third-party hosting, form processing and email services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Customer information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for order processing and business purposes. Customers may contact us to request deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or personal information, please contact J S Computer Embroidery Works through the business contact details provided on our website.
